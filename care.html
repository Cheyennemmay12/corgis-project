Corgis bring energy and opinions to the day. A thoughtful routine gives both a good place to go.

The everyday essentials

Room to move, time to connect.

Every dog is an individual. Build a plan with your veterinarian around your corgi's age, health, and temperament.

01

Move and explore

Offer regular walks, sniffing time, and play. Mix physical activity with simple training games and puzzle activities to engage a clever, busy mind.

↗
02

Brush the double coat

Routine brushing helps manage shedding and keeps the coat comfortable. Check ears, trim nails as needed, and ask a groomer or veterinarian to show you safe techniques.

✳
03

Keep training kind

Short, reward-based sessions make good use of a corgi's focus. Start early with social experiences, everyday manners, and consistent household routines.

↗
04

Support a healthy body

Measure meals, keep fresh water available, and ask your veterinarian about an appropriate diet and body condition. A healthy weight matters for every dog, including long-backed breeds.

✳
05

Stay ahead of health

Keep up with routine veterinary visits, dental care, parasite prevention, and recommended screening. Your vet can help you spot changes early and tailor advice to your dog.

↗

A thoughtful start

Choose the right match,
not just the right look.

Meet adult dogs and puppies with a responsible breeder or rescue. Ask about health history, temperament, and the day-to-day needs of the particular dog in front of you.
